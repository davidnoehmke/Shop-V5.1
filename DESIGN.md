# LEAFerservice storefront design contract

## Brand overview
LEAFerservice connects plants, light, vessels, substrates and knowledge into understandable indoor plant systems. The storefront is for people who want a calm, designed home while still making well-informed care decisions.

## Design thesis
A quiet botanical room is the map: every object opens a clear next step.

## Colors
- Ink green #183c31: primary text, navigation and key actions.
- Deep green #244c3c: primary interactive surface.
- Sage #698267: editorial emphasis and secondary botanical tone.
- Soft lime #dbeaa9: selective call-to-action emphasis.
- Paper #f7f6ef: primary page surface.
- Mist #edeee5: quiet navigation and card surface.
- Line #dce1d6: gentle structural boundaries.

## Typography
- Display and interface: Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial, medium to bold, compact negative tracking for large headings.
- Editorial accent: Georgia, serif, only for the second line of the home statement and long-form reading moments.
- Body: inherited system sans, 14–17px, 1.6–1.7 line-height.

## Spacing and composition
- Main page rail: maximum 1320px with 20px minimum mobile gutters.
- Room homepage: image and statement share a two-column desktop composition and collapse to statement first on mobile.
- Homepage welcome: the merchant's people-and-plants image leads the entry, beside a short personal statement on desktop and above it on mobile. Keep faces and hands visible, with all copy outside the image. Use the established Helvetica stack and the editorial Georgia accent for the second heading line. The main action opens the assortment; the quiet secondary link opens the growing calendar. Image, alt text, captions, copy and links remain merchant-editable in the existing homepage section.
- Use 18–24px radii on large interactive surfaces and 100px radii only for primary pills.
- Product or topic groups live on their own landingpage, never as a second competing home-page route.

## Components
- Primary action: deep green pill, white text, compact arrow.
- Secondary action: text-only green link with arrow.
- Room hotspot: light frosted pill, numbered dark-green disc and concise label; icon-only on small screens with an accessible label.
- World tab: structured, equal-width link beneath the room; no local product-panel expansion on the homepage.
- Product cards: live only inside their corresponding world landingpage.

## Dos and don'ts
- Do use real store imagery and clear labels around every jump point.
- Do preserve direct, indexable collection and article routes.
- Do keep the room as the primary discovery mechanism.
- Do not duplicate world product cards on the homepage.
- Do not use decorative motion that interferes with reading or selection.
- Do not replace accessible links with click-only elements.

## Motion philosophy
Use only short transform, background and shadow transitions with a decelerating curve. Press feedback is tactile and subtle. All motion is disabled when the shopper requests reduced motion.
